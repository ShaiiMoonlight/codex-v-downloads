Codex V

A writing app for novelists, for Windows and Android.

Codex V keeps your manuscript together with everything around it: characters, places, lore, timelines, maps, plot arcs and modules you design yourself. Your projects are stored on your own device first, and work without an internet connection.

What it does

  • A chapter editor with history, focus mode and find on page.
  • Notes for characters, places and lore, linked to the chapters they appear in.
  • Importing a manuscript from Word, PDF, text or Markdown, with a review of the chapters and names it finds.
  • Optional sync between your own devices, through your Codex V account.
  • Optional AI help, using your own API key, which is off until you turn it on.
  • Optional Google Drive connection, to bring a Google Doc in as chapters. It only sees the files you pick.
