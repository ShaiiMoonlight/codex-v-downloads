Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Codex V is a writing app. Your projects are stored on your own device. This page says what leaves your device, where it goes, and how to stop it. Codex V has no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry.

On your device

Your projects, chapters, notes, imported documents and settings are stored on your device. API keys and sign-in tokens are encrypted with your operating system's keystore and used only by the app itself.

Sync (optional)

If you create a Codex V account and sign in, your email address and your projects are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, so they can be copied to your other devices. Without an account, nothing is synced. You can delete projects from your account at any time, and ask for your account to be deleted through the contact link below.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Codex V asks Google for one permission, drive.file. That lets it see only the files you choose in Google's own file picker, or that Codex V itself created. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

  • What it reads: the content and basic details (name, version, last change) of the Google Docs and files you link, so it can import them as chapters and bring in later changes.
  • Where it goes: the content is stored in your project on your device, and synced to your account only if you use sync. It is not sent to anyone else.
  • Your Google sign-in: the token that keeps you connected is encrypted and stays on the device you connected. It is never synced or sent to us.
  • AI: text from Google Drive is treated like the rest of your manuscript. It is only sent to an AI service if you have turned AI on yourself (see below).
  • Disconnecting in Codex V revokes its access. You can also remove it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Codex V's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by people, except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires it.

AI features (optional, off by default)

AI is off until you turn it on, and each feature has its own switch. When it's on, the text that feature needs is sent to Anthropic (Claude) with your own API key. Voice calls, also off by default, send your voice to Deepgram and the replies to ElevenLabs with your own keys. Each service's own terms apply to what it receives. The app shows exactly what is sent under Settings › AI › What's sent where.

Updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions. That shows GitHub your IP address. Nothing from your projects is sent.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data. It only goes to the services above, and only for the features you use.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
